Load a CSV to get started

This page starts empty. Your data stays in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.
  1. Click Load CSV in the top right.
  2. Select your exported transactions CSV file.
  3. The dashboard will render KPIs, charts, and the transactions table automatically.
Where to get a CSV (example: Trade Republic)
You can use an export from Trade Republic (or a similar broker) as long as it contains a header row with the columns listed below.
  • Open the Trade Republic app
  • Go to Profile / Settings → Documents (wording may vary by app version)
  • Select your Account statement / Transactions export and download the CSV
  • Then click Load CSV here and select the downloaded file
Note: menu names can differ slightly depending on your region/app version.
CSV requirements
The parser expects a header row with at least a date column. Other supported columns include amount, category, type, asset_class, name, symbol, shares, price, fee, tax, description, counterparty_name, and transaction_id.
